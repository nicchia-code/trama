# Show the Fix options group and the Note field in the Drift comparison of the Diagram and the Board

In the right column of the comparison, between the What should happen text and its Keep this, a group of radio buttons headed Fix options offers the options in their order, each with its title in bold and its text as Markdown, the recommended one marked and selected at first, changed by clicking or with the arrow keys. Under it a multi-line field labelled Note (optional) takes free text. A Drift without options shows neither the group nor the heading, only the field. The Board card and the Diagram's details show the same, and the left column has neither.
