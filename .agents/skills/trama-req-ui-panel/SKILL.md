# Requirements: Interfaccia, Interfaccia generale e pannello

Generated by Trama from `.trama/requirements/ui/panel.md`. Do not edit this file: change the Requirements through a Trama Proposal (or ask the developer).

These are the developer's Requirements for how the System is built. Follow them when you write code. The Model (`.trama/model/`) says what the System does; when a Requirement and the Model disagree, stop and tell the developer.
A section titled with a Model reference (such as `order/Paid`) applies to that element and to everything inside it.

## General

- Dove il pannello o la riga di comando nominano una Proposal, un Task o un Drift con il suo ID (schede della Board e righe dentro una scheda di design, badge `blocked` e `waiting`, dettagli nel Diagramma, `trama board`, i comandi `list`, `show` e `next`, i messaggi dei comandi e i testi dei pulsanti, per esempio «Approves Proposal P3 and changes the Model to match»), mostrano il numero breve (`P3`, `T3`, `D3`) se la scheda ne ha uno e, solo se non ne ha (scheda chiusa, o Task Done senza numero, vedi `rules`), la sua identità permanente di otto caratteri. Mai tutti e due insieme. Il titolo resta accanto, come prima.
- Nel pannello il numero breve e l'identità permanente stanno nello stesso punto, con lo stesso carattere a spaziatura fissa; li distinguono il tooltip e il nome accessibile, «Short number (temporary)» o «Permanent ID», così il modo in cui sono scritti non è l'unico segnale.

## panel

- All prose the panel shows is rendered as Markdown, in every tab: the descriptions of the System, Features, Machines, States, Actors and Events in the Diagram's details, the body of a Proposal, the introduction of an Area, la descrizione di un frammento and the text of every Requirement section (also the earlier text of a changed section), the texts of a Drift and the body of a Task. Titles, IDs, labels and button texts stay plain. Where an Event description sits in a one-line list entry, only inline formatting is rendered.
- Supported: paragraphs, headings, bold, italic, inline code, fenced code blocks, bulleted and numbered lists (nested too), block quotes and links. Not supported: tables and images; what is not supported shows as written. Raw HTML is never interpreted: it shows as text.
- A link opens in the system browser through the editor only when it is an http or https address, and its tooltip shows the address. Any other link shows its label as plain text.
- Headings inside a rendered text are never larger than the heading of the element that holds the text, so a text cannot compete with the panel's own headings. Colours and fonts follow the editor's theme; code uses the editor's monospace font.
- Rendered text never makes the panel scroll sideways: long words wrap and a long line of a code block scrolls inside its own block. Text stays selectable.
- Malformed Markdown never prevents a text from showing: it is shown as written, as far as it cannot be read as Markdown.
- L'overlay delle domande segue le stesse regole: il testo della domanda, il contesto, la descrizione e i dettagli di ogni opzione sono resi come Markdown; le etichette delle opzioni, i pulsanti e la risposta libera mostrata nel riepilogo restano testo semplice.

## panel/Board

- A design card shows the Proposal's title, a counter of finished Tasks over all its Tasks (for example "2/5 done") and the Run this design button. It is closed when the Board first shows and stays as the developer left it while the Board refreshes during the session; closing the panel forgets which cards were open.
- An expanded design card lists its Tasks in creation order. Each sub-item shows the Task's title and its status dot, with no move buttons.
- Proposal, Drift e Task delle colonne To decide, To do e In progress stanno in ordine di creazione. Una scheda di design sta dove sta il suo primo Task.
- La colonna Done sta in ordine inverso di fine: prima la scheda finita più di recente. La data di fine di una scheda di Task è il suo `finished`; quella di una scheda di design è il `finished` più recente fra i suoi Task. A parità di istante decide l'ordine di creazione inverso. Dentro una scheda di design i Task restano in ordine di creazione.
- Le schede della colonna Done sono raggruppate per giorno di fine, nell'ora locale della macchina del developer. Ogni gruppo ha un'intestazione con l'etichetta e il numero di schede del gruppo: `Today · 3`, `Yesterday · 5`, poi la data scritta per esteso nella lingua dell'editor (per esempio `September 12, 2026 · 2`). Un gruppo senza schede non compare.
- In cima alla colonna Done un menu a tendina sceglie il periodo: `Today`, `Last 7 days`, `Last 30 days`, `All`. `Today` è il giorno locale in corso; `Last 7 days` e `Last 30 days` contano oggi e i 6 o 29 giorni locali precedenti. Il periodo iniziale è `Last 7 days`. La scelta resta mentre la Board si aggiorna durante la sessione e si dimentica alla chiusura del pannello. Il menu ha un'etichetta accessibile, «Done period».
- Una scheda di design entra nel periodo se la sua data di fine ci cade. Le schede Done senza data di fine (un Task senza `finished`, o un design in cui nessun Task ha `finished`) stanno in un ultimo gruppo, `No date`, mostrato solo con `All`.
- Il numero accanto al titolo della colonna Done conta le schede che il periodo scelto lascia vedere. Quando il periodo non lascia vedere nessuna scheda, la colonna dice `Nothing done in this period`.
- The Board offers no way to move, drag, take back or remove a card: no move arrows, no dragging and no Take back button on a Task card, on a Task row of a design card or on a design card, and no button that deletes a card. The Board draws no state for a move in progress. Moves made from the command line appear when the Board next receives the Tasks. Looking and opening stay: links to the Diagram, expanding and collapsing design cards, "Show details", choosing the period of the Done column, and closing notices.
- Every Task card, and every Task row of a design card, starts with a status dot, to the left of the Task's ID or title. The dot tells To do (hollow, muted), In progress (warning colour) and Done (green) apart by colour, and carries a text label (tooltip and accessible name) with the column's name, so the colour is never the only signal.
- The dot of a Task taken by the agent is in the accent colour and pulses; its label is "Agent working". The pulse stops when the system asks for reduced motion, leaving a still dot in the accent colour. No Task shows an "agent working" badge; no other dot is animated.
- Un Task bloccato, sulla propria scheda o dentro una scheda di design, mostra un badge `blocked` con l'id del Drift, oppure `waiting` con l'id del Task di correzione. Il badge porta a quel Drift sul Diagramma, o alla scheda di quel Task. Ha un tooltip e un nome accessibile che dicono perché il Task è bloccato, così il colore non è l'unico segnale. Il badge non disabilita nessun controllo.
- Inside a design card, a Task taken by the agent shows the same pulsing dot on its own row. Run this design and Run all To do are disabled, with a tooltip naming the Task or saying that an agent is working, while a relevant Task is taken.
- Run all To do is also disabled when every Task in To do is blocked, and Run this design when every Task of that design in To do is blocked; the tooltip says that all the Tasks are blocked and names the Drift or the fix Task of the first one. When both reasons hold, the tooltip gives the agent working.
- A card the agent announced is drawn as a ghost in the column it will land in: To decide for a Proposal or a Drift, To do for a Task. The ghost is a skeleton of a card: a dashed border, translucent, with a shimmer on its text lines. The shimmer stops when the system asks for reduced motion, leaving the still skeleton.
- The ghost shows the kind of the announced card ("Proposal", "Task" or "Drift"), its title and one line telling the phase: "Preparing" until a Draft exists, "Writing" once the Draft exists, and "Waiting for your answers" instead of either while a question round is open. The title is the developer's request until the Draft exists, then the Draft's title; both are updated by themselves as the files change, without the developer doing anything.
- The ghost stands after the real cards of its kind in its column (Proposals before Drifts in To decide), and the real card takes its place when it arrives so that no other card jumps. Several ghosts keep the order in which they were announced.
- The ghost cannot be clicked, focused or dragged and has no buttons or links. It has an accessible name that says the kind, the title and the phase, for example "Proposal being written by the agent: <title>". It is not counted: the number of the To decide column, the number of cards of any other column and the number on the Board tab leave it out.
- When an announcement is withdrawn because the agent's command ended, its terminal was closed or fifteen minutes passed without anything happening, the ghost is removed and a notice appears at the top of the Board: "The agent finished without delivering <kind> <title>.", one notice per ghost, with a button to close it. A notice stays until the developer closes it or the panel is closed. A ghost whose Draft the developer discarded is removed with no notice.
- The ghosts and the notices are drawn also while another tab is shown, so they are current when the Board is shown again; closing and opening the panel brings back the ghosts that still exist.
- A Task card that is not inside a design card shows no excerpt of the Task's body. It has a toggle, "Show details", that expands the whole body as Markdown in the card and then reads "Hide details"; it is hidden when the Task has no body. The card is closed when the Board first shows and stays as the developer left it while the Board refreshes during the session; closing the panel forgets which cards were open. The toggle names the Task for assistive technology and reports whether it is expanded.
- Task rows inside a design card have no toggle and show no body.

- The Board shows no Proposal the agent is still writing as a card; only its ghost is drawn.
- The Diagram's Proposal selector and the Requirements tab list only handed-over (open) Proposals.

## panel/Requirements

- Ogni Area mostra titolo, descrizione e introduzione, poi i suoi frammenti nell'ordine dei nomi dei file; ogni frammento è un gruppo con il titolo, il nome del file (`<frammento>.md`) in testo secondario, la descrizione e sotto le sue sezioni. La sezione General, in qualunque frammento stia, compare per prima nel suo frammento.
- Un'Area senza frammenti mostra, sotto la descrizione, la riga `No fragments.`
- Con una Proposal selezionata, i frammenti seguono le stesse etichette delle Aree: `new fragment` per uno nuovo, `fragment removed` per uno cancellato, nessuna per uno cambiato; le sezioni dentro tengono le loro (`added`, `removed`, `changed`). Una sezione spostata da un frammento a un altro compare solo nel frammento di arrivo, con l'etichetta `moved from <frammento>`, seguita da `changed` e dal testo di prima se anche il testo cambia.
