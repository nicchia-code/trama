# Editor panel

The single Trama panel in the editor, where the developer looks at the Model and decides. Ha sei viste: la Board con ciò che attende una decisione e i Task, il Diagramma del Modello, i Requisiti, le Specs, la History del lavoro fatto e le impostazioni di Trama. With origin on GitHub it has a seventh view, Team: the pull requests to check, for everyone (see the PR Check), and, with Manager mode on, the people of the Manager's team and their work (see Manager mode). Il developer le sceglie dal menu laterale di Trama, nella barra laterale dell'editor, al posto di una barra delle schede del pannello. Board, Diagramma, Requisiti, Specs e History sono viste in sola lettura: ciò che il developer cambia nel Modello e nel lavoro passa dalle decisioni su Proposal e Drift e dai pulsanti che avviano l'agente. The Settings tab and the Team view are the only places of the panel where the developer changes something directly: the settings of Trama, in his user settings, and the data of the people of his team, in his editor. Tasks cannot be moved, dragged or taken back from the panel, and the only way to remove them is Clear in the Done column, which deletes the finished Tasks for good: the Board only shows them, and they change column only through the agent's work, the decision on a Drift or the command line.
