# Refuse to approve, reject or accept through a Drift a Proposal still being written, and let the developer discard a Draft

Approving, rejecting or accepting through a Drift the attached Proposal when it is a Draft is refused saying the Proposal is still being written; the Drift stays open. Discarding turns a Draft into a discarded one, needs no feedback and appends nothing to the Proposal; discarding a Proposal that is not a Draft is refused. Proposals that require the discarded one and the Drift it is attached to are left as they are.
