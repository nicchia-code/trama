# Requirements: Riga di comando, Unify Drifts nella riga di comando

Usa quando cambi `trama drift new` con `unifies` e `differences`, ciò che `drift show`, `drift list`, `drift edit`, `drift move` e `project show` dicono e fanno dei Drift unificati, delle loro differenze e dei Drift uniti, o le decisioni per differenza di `proposal edit`.

## agent.UNIFY_DRIFTS

- `drift new` accepts `unifies`: a list of Drifts, each as a permanent identity or short number, converted before writing. With `unifies` it requires `differences`: a list of `{ drift, title, refs, happens, should, where, options?, proposal?, linked? }`, where `drift` (permanent identity or short number) names the Drift, or the difference of a gathered unified Drift, it comes from, `options` follows the shape of `drift new` and `linked` lists the `drift` of other differences. With `unifies`, `task`, `previous`, `refs`, `happens`, `should`, `where`, `options` and `proposal` at the top level are a usage error naming the field, and so is `differences` without `unifies`. `dryRun: true` with `unifies` prints the Tasks the unified Drift would block, origins of the unified Drifts included, without writing anything.
- Un rifiuto esce con 1 e nomina ogni problema in una riga (per esempio `Drift D3 is part of resolution <id>`); non scrive nulla.
- Riuscito, stampa l'identità del Drift nuovo come ogni `drift new` e una riga per ogni Drift unito, `Unified <identità> into <identità del nuovo>`; con `--json` aggiunge `unifies` e `differences`.
- `drift show` of a unified Drift with differences shows, after the title, one block per difference in order: a line `Difference <short number or identity of its Drift> <title>` followed by `open` or its decision (`fix code: <option title>` with the fix Task, `adapt model`, `dismissed`), a line `Linked to …` when linked, and then, for an open difference, its two sides, where in the code, its fix options and its attached Proposal, as for a Drift; then the "Unifies" block. A unified Drift without differences is shown as today. `drift show` of a unified Drift has, after its sides or its differences, a block with the heading "Unifies" and one line per unified Drift (`<identità> <titolo>`); con più precedenti una riga `Follows Drift …` per ciascuno, nell'ordine di `previous`. `drift show` di un Drift unito ha, subito dopo il titolo, la riga `Unified into <numero breve o identità> <titolo>`, followed, once its difference is decided, by that decision.
- `drift list` and `project show` mark an open unified Drift `(unifies <N>)`, and one with differences `(unifies <N>, <K> open)`; con `all`, `drift list` segna un Drift unito `(unified into <numero breve o identità>)`.
- `drift move` su un Drift unito è rifiutato come su ogni Drift risolto. On a unified Drift with differences it follows `rules#agent.UNIFY_DRIFTS`.
- `drift edit` on a unified Drift with differences accepts `prepared` as `{ questions: [{ difference, question, decisions }] }` and `proposal` as `{ difference, proposal }`, as `tech#agent.UNIFY_DRIFTS` says; a single `prepared.question` on such a Drift is a usage error.
- `proposal edit` with `drifts` accepts `difference` on each entry (the `drift` of a difference, as a permanent identity or short number); an entry on a unified Drift with differences without `difference`, or with `difference` on any other Drift, is refused naming the entry. `proposal show` and its `--json` list the decisions per difference.
- `docs/cli.md` e il `--help` di `drift new` descrivono `unifies` e `differences`.
