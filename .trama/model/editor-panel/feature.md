# Editor panel

The single Trama panel in the editor, where the developer looks at the Model and decides. It has three tabs: the Diagram of the Model, the Board with what awaits a decision and the Tasks, and the Requirements. The Diagram, the Board and the Requirements are read-only views; everything the developer changes goes through decisions on Proposals and Drifts and through buttons that start the agent. Tasks cannot be moved, dragged, taken back or removed from the panel: the Board only shows them, and they change column only through the agent's work, the decision on a Drift or the command line.
