## Codicons

The generic technology icon in the Specs tab is the `package` glyph of the Codicons icon set (`@vscode/codicons`, the icon font of the editor), copied as an SVG path. The glyphs of the same set on the panel's buttons and badges (`add`, `play`, `wand`, `search`, `type-hierarchy`, `checklist`, `check`, `close`, `circle-slash`, `go-to-file`, `info`, `chevron-down`, `chevron-up`, `chevron-right`, `chevron-left`, `link`, `settings-gear`) are copied the same way; `settings-gear` is scaled from its 24-unit source to the 16-unit grid.
