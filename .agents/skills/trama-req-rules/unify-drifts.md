# Requirements: Regole di business, Unify Drifts

Usa quando implementi Unify Drifts: quali Drift si uniscono, quando si può lanciare, l'istruzione all'agente, ciò che l'agente fa, le regole del Drift unificato e delle sue differenze, dei Drift uniti e della loro risoluzione.

## developer.UNIFY_DRIFTS

- Un Drift è idoneo quando è aperto e non sta in nessuna Proposal di risoluzione in scrittura o aperta, comprese quelle con `prepared: true`. Unify Drifts unisce tutti i Drift idonei, nell'ordine di creazione, raccolti nel momento in cui l'istruzione parte davvero. A unified Drift with differences is eligible only while none of its differences is decided.
- Unify Drifts è indisponibile, con questi motivi in quest'ordine: `Drifts are being resolved` (un lavoro di risoluzione o di unificazione lanciato da questa finestra è in corso, o è aperta una domanda pronta), `A run is in progress` (un Run lanciato da Trama non è finito), `Proposal <id> resolving Drifts awaits a decision` (la più vecchia risoluzione in scrittura o aperta senza `prepared: true`), `Unify needs at least two open Drifts` (meno di due Drift idonei). Una scheda che aspetta la fine del turno del suo agente non conta.
- L'istruzione non nomina una skill. È il testo di istruzioni di Unify Drifts che Trama porta con sé, la cui prima riga è `Trama: Unify Drifts`, seguito da una riga sola: "Unify the open Drifts `a`, `b`, `c` into one Drift, following the instructions above." con le identità permanenti dei Drift raccolti in ordine di creazione, e poi, sulla stessa riga, le frasi di `rules#agent-terminal` nel loro ordine (limite dei sotto-agenti, lingua, domande). Il testo arriva come file allegato al primo messaggio, la riga come prompt.
- The instruction text tells the agent, in English, to:
  - read every gathered Drift with `trama drift show` and the code at the places each one cites;
  - ask the developer nothing, neither with `trama question new` nor with its own question tool: Unify Drifts gathers, it decides nothing;
  - check quickly how the Drifts depend on one another: two differences are linked when fixing the code of one changes, undoes or needs what fixing the other does, or when they change the same place in the code in different ways; Drifts that only concern the same area are not linked;
  - write one difference per gathered Drift, in their creation order, with that Drift's title, refs, what happens, what should happen, where in the code and fix options as they are, rewriting them only where a linked difference requires it so that the fix options of each difference stay coherent with the other differences; a gathered Drift that is itself a unified Drift with differences gives each of its differences as they are;
  - keep, for each difference, the Proposal attached to its Drift; only when that Proposal no longer fits because of a linked difference, or the rewritten difference has no fix option, begin a new one that takes over what is needed from it, and hand it over right after the Drift, as for any Drift with a Proposal;
  - register everything with one single `trama drift new` that carries `unifies`, `differences` with their `linked`, and Trama's announcement;
  - with the Async Drift Resolver on, prepare at the end the prepared round of the unified Drift (`rules#drift-resolution/Answering a prepared question`): for each difference, start from the prepared question of its Drift when it was still valid and change its context and options only where the linked differences require it, saying in the context which differences it is linked to and what each option implies for them; write a new question, as the `trama-resolve-drifts` skill says, only for a Drift without a valid one;
  - not decide, resolve, close or discard any Drift or Proposal in any other way, not write a resolution and not start Resolve; at the end tell the developer to press `Resolve` on the unified Drift.
- Il lavoro di unificazione conta come un lavoro di risoluzione per ogni esclusione di `rules#drift-resolution`: finché è in corso nessun Run, nessun Resolve, nessun Rework with feedback, nessun altro Unify Drifts e nessun Dismiss Drift dal pannello parte, con gli stessi motivi. Finisce come un lavoro di risoluzione.
- Se l'istruzione non parte davvero non nasce nessun lavoro, nessun annuncio resta e nessuna scheda è segnata.

## agent.UNIFY_DRIFTS

- `trama drift new` with `unifies` files the unified Drift. It is refused, writing nothing and naming every problem, when: `unifies` has fewer than two Drifts or a repeated Drift; a named Drift does not exist, is not open, is in a resolution Proposal being written or open, or is a unified Drift with a decided difference; `differences` does not have exactly one entry per difference the gathered Drifts give (one per Drift, or one per difference of a unified Drift with differences), in their order; a difference breaks a rule of a new Drift's sections and options (`rules#drift`), or has no fix options and no `proposal`; a `linked` name is not another difference of the same Drift; a difference's `proposal` is neither the Proposal attached to its Drift (or to that difference) nor a Draft or open Proposal attached to no open Drift; `task` or `previous` is given, which with `unifies` Trama computes.
- Le origini del Drift unificato sono le origini dei Drift uniti, senza doppioni, nell'ordine di `unifies`. I precedenti sono i precedenti dei Drift uniti, più ogni Drift di cui un'origine è il Task di correzione, senza doppioni, nello stesso ordine. Il Drift è ricorrente se ne ha almeno uno. Each difference keeps the origins and precedents of its own Drift.
- In the same step every unified Drift becomes `resolved` with `resolution: unified` and remembers the unified Drift; it loses its short number; the Tasks it blocked are now blocked by the unified Drift; its prepared question no longer counts. Its attached Proposal, being written or open, stays attached when its difference names it, and is otherwise discarded without feedback.
- A Drift `unified` whose difference is still open does not count among the already resolved Drifts when deciding whether to file a difference, cannot be the `previous` of any Drift and refuses every `drift move`, as any closed Drift. Once its difference is decided it counts as a Drift resolved with that difference's decision, on that difference's refs: a difference closed without changes holds back filing the same difference again as a dismissed Drift does (`rules#drift`), and a difference decided `fix-code` or `proposal` may be named as `previous`.
- Correggere il codice di un Drift con più origini rimanda in To do ogni origine non Done, che aspetta il Task di correzione; accettare la Proposal o chiudere il Drift lascia ogni origine dov'è. For a unified Drift with differences this holds difference by difference: only the origins of a difference fixed in the code go back to To do, and they wait for its fix Task.
- Il modello scelto per una Proposal vale per Resolve su un Drift unificato solo se tutte le sue origini appartengono a quella Proposal; for a unified Drift with differences, the origins of its open differences.
- A unified Drift with differences blocks Tasks (`rules#task`) only with the refs and origins of its open differences.
- `trama drift move` on a unified Drift with differences: `code-to-be-fixed` and `proposal-accepted` are refused with `Drift <id> has differences: decide them with Resolve`; `dismissed` closes every open difference with `decision: dismissed` and is refused, as for a Drift with an attached Proposal, when an open difference has one. Dismiss Drift from the panel closes every open difference with `decision: dismissed` and discards their attached Proposals without feedback. Both leave the decided differences as they are.

## drift-resolution/Resolving

- For a unified Drift with differences, Resolve Drifts asks, in its one round, one single-choice question per open difference, with `id` the difference's `drift`, following the rules of the question of a Drift on that difference: one option per fix option of the difference in their order and one for the behaviour the code already has, or, without fix options, adopting its attached Proposal (recommended) and closing without changes; for a difference from a merge Drift the code's behaviour is offered and recommended (`rules#drift-resolution`). The context names the linked differences and what each choice implies for them.
- When the answers give linked differences different decisions (a `fix-code` with any option counts as one decision), the agent reconciles them before writing: it may ask once more, in one round, only the questions of those linked differences, with options adapted to the answers already given. Unanswered and skipped differences stay out of the resolution and open.
- The resolution lists one decision per answered difference; for `proposal` it carries the changes of the Proposal attached to that difference, adapted to the other decisions when needed.

## drift-resolution/Answering a prepared question

- A question of the prepared round of a unified Drift with differences is valid when its difference is open, the question reads without errors, the fingerprint of the text of the Model and the Requirements on that difference's refs is the one recorded, every `fix-code` names an existing fix option of that difference and a `proposal` decision has the Proposal attached to that difference being written or open. The code does not count.
- `Resolve` opens the prepared round at once, without an agent, only when every open difference has a valid question; the round holds those questions in the order of the differences, recommendations preselected. When some open difference has none, `Resolve` launches the resolution agent, which keeps the valid questions as they are, prepares the others, rewrites the section and asks one round about every open difference.
- From the answers Trama writes the resolution itself when at least one question is answered, every answered question has a fix option (its free text becomes the note) or, without free text, `dismissed` or `proposal`, and no two linked differences got different decisions. The resolution has `kind: resolution`, `prepared: true`, the title `Resolve: <title of the Drift>`, one body line per decided difference and one entry per answered difference, copying for each `proposal` the files of the Proposal attached to that difference.
- Otherwise (free text with a choice that is not a fix option, free text without a choice, linked differences decided differently, or a refused delivery of the resolution Trama wrote) Trama launches the resolution agent with every answer. With linked differences decided differently the agent reconciles them as in `rules#drift-resolution/Resolving`; otherwise it asks nothing again.
- Skipped questions apply no recommendation: their differences stay open with their questions prepared. When every question is skipped nothing is written.

## drift/Differences decided

- Approving a resolution gives each listed difference its decision. `fix-code` differences get one fix Task each, except differences linked to each other, directly or through other linked differences fixed in the same resolution, which share one fix Task: its refs are the union of theirs, its body carries, difference by difference, the two sides, where in the code, the chosen option and the note, and it is a Technical Task when all its refs are Requirements. The Task belongs to the design of the origins as for any fix Task, when they all belong to the same one. `proposal` differences are adapted by the resolution's changes; `dismissed` ones change nothing. The attached Proposal of every decided difference is set aside as for a resolved Drift.
- The unified Drift becomes Differences decided, losing its short number and its card, in the same step that decides its last open difference; until then it stays Open with its card. A fix Task closed without changes later changes only the `decision` of its differences.
