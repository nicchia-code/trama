# Show Diagram descriptions and Proposal bodies as Markdown

In the Diagram's details, the descriptions of the System, Features, Machines, States and Actors, and the body of a Proposal, are shown formatted as Markdown. Event descriptions in list entries show inline formatting only. Titles, IDs, labels and button texts stay plain.
