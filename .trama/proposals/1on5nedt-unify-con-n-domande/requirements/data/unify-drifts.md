# Dati di Unify Drifts

## agent.UNIFY_DRIFTS
- Il front matter del Drift unificato contiene `unifies`: la lista delle identità permanenti dei Drift che unisce, nell'ordine di creazione di quei Drift, senza doppioni. Si scrive alla creazione e non cambia più; è assente in ogni altro Drift.
- Nel Drift unificato `origin` e `previous` sono una stringa quando c'è un solo valore e una lista quando ce n'è più d'uno; assenti quando non ce n'è nessuno. Ogni Drift si legge con entrambe le forme, senza riscriverlo; una lista vuota vale come assente.
- La Snapshot e i `--json` di `drift show`, `drift list` e `project show` portano `unifies` (lista, vuota se assente) e `unifiedInto` (identità o `null`), e `origin` e `previous` sempre come liste, vuote se assenti.
- A unified Drift written with differences has `differences` in its front matter: one entry per Drift of `unifies`, in the same order, each an object `{ drift, refs, proposal?, linked?, decision?, option?, note?, task?, resolvedBy? }`. `drift` is the permanent identity of the Drift the difference comes from and identifies the difference everywhere (questions, decisions, links). `refs` are the references of the difference, each also in the Drift's own `refs`. `proposal` is the Proposal attached to that difference, or absent. `linked` lists the `drift` of the other differences it is linked to; the relation is symmetric and every name must be a difference of the same Drift. An open difference has no `decision`. A decided one has `decision` (`fix-code`, `proposal` or `dismissed`), `resolvedBy` (the resolution that decided it, absent when Dismiss Drift did) and, for `fix-code`, `option`, `note` when written, and `task`, the fix Task it shares with the linked differences fixed together.
- The Drift's own `refs` are the union of the `refs` of its differences, in their order, without duplicates. Its own `proposal`, `option`, `note` and `task` stay absent. The origins and precedents of a difference are those of the Drift it comes from, read from that Drift's file, which keeps them.
- The body of a unified Drift with differences has, in place of the four sections of `data#drift`, one block per difference in the order of `differences`: a heading `## Difference <drift>: <title>`, then `### What happens`, `### What should happen`, `### Where in the code` and `### Fix options`, whose options are `####` headings followed by their text, with the same rules and limits as the sections and options of a Drift; a difference without fix options has no `### Fix options` and has `proposal`. `## Prepared question`, when present, comes after all the blocks.
- A difference closed by its fix Task closed without changes has `decision: dismissed` and keeps `task`, `option`, `note` and `resolvedBy` as they were after the choice to fix the code, as a Drift does (`data#drift`).
- A unified Drift without `differences`, written before they existed, keeps its four sections and is read as today, as a Drift with one difference; it is never rewritten to add them.
- The Snapshot and the `--json` of `drift show` carry `differences` (empty list when absent): for each `{ drift, title, refs, sides, where, options, proposal, linked, decision, option, note, task, open }`; `drift list` and `project show` carry `differences` as the counts `{ total, open }`, or `null` when absent.

## drift/Unified
- Un Drift unito ha `status: resolved`, `resolution: unified` e `unifiedInto`: l'identità permanente del Drift unificato in cui è confluito. Si scrivono nello stesso passo della creazione del Drift unificato e non cambiano più. Gli altri campi restano com'erano, `proposal` compreso, anche se quella Proposal è stata scartata; non ha `task`, `option`, `note` né `resolvedBy`.
- `unifiedInto` è assente in ogni altro Drift; un Drift con `resolution: unified` senza `unifiedInto`, o con `unifiedInto` e un'altra `resolution`, è un errore di lettura.
- The outcome of the difference of a Drift Unified is never written in its own file: it is read from the difference with the same `drift` in the unified Drift that `unifiedInto` names, following the chain when that Drift was unified again.

## drift/Differences decided
- A unified Drift with differences is written `status: resolved` with `resolution: differences` in the same write that decides its last open difference; `resolution: differences` without `differences`, or with an open difference, is a read error.

## drift-resolution/Answering a prepared question
- The `## Prepared question` of a unified Drift with differences holds one `json` object `{ questions, prepared }`: `questions` lists one entry per difference that had a question when it was written, in the order of the differences, each `{ difference, question, decisions, fingerprint }`. `question` follows `data#question-round` with `id` equal to `difference` and `multi: false`; `decisions` and `fingerprint` are as in `data#drift-resolution`, with the decisions naming the fix options and the attached Proposal of that difference and the fingerprint computed on the `refs` of that difference. A difference decided later keeps its entry, which no longer counts. An unreadable section counts as absent, as for any Drift; one entry with a different shape makes only that question unreadable.
- In a resolution (`data#proposal`), an entry of `drifts` that decides a difference also carries `difference`, the `drift` of that difference. A unified Drift with differences appears only in entries with `difference`, at most one per difference and only for differences that are open; every other Drift appears in at most one entry without `difference`, as today.
