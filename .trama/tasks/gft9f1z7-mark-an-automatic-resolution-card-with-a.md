# Mark an automatic resolution card with a textual delegation indicator

While open, an automatic resolution stays the ordinary aggregated resolution card and also carries a text indicator that it was prepared by the developer's delegation. Its approval controls and blocker reasons stay usable when automatic approval does not go ahead. Once approved it leaves To decide as any resolution does.
