# Animate Diagram view on steps

When a step of a focus is taken on the Diagram, the view slides until it contains all the elements of that step, with a short animation. The zoom never goes beyond the limit of Fit. With reduced motion requested by the system, the view jumps at once. Fit still fits the whole level and does not change the focus.
