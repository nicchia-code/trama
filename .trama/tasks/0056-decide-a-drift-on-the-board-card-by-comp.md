# Decide a Drift on the Board card by comparing its two sides

The Drift card in the To decide column shows the same comparison, buttons and consequence lines as the Diagram's detail panel, and keeps its link to the Diagram or to the Requirement. The card does not offer separate Fix code, Accept Proposal or Dismiss buttons.
