# Scan is off during Runs text

The text that explains why Scan is disabled with the Advisor Scanner on is now Scan is off during Runs. It appears in the Scan tooltip of the Diagram bar at every level, in the accessible name of every disabled Scan button and in the small line under Scan System in the side menu. The text lives in one constant, SCANS_OFF_TOOLTIP, and is always in English.
