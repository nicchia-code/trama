# Show Requirements, introductions and earlier texts as Markdown

In the Requirements tab, an Area's introduction, the text of every Requirement section and the earlier text of a changed section are shown formatted as Markdown, so lists of Requirements read as lists. The Area's description line, titles and badges stay plain.
