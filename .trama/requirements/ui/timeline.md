# Timeline and signals

## issue/Published
- Status pills of a published issue in the Issues tab, each with its icon and colour of the theme's palette: `Open` (green, lucide `CircleDot`), `In progress` (blue, lucide `LoaderCircle`, still), `In review` (amber, lucide `Eye`), `Closed` (violet, lucide `CircleCheck`), `Not planned` (muted, lucide `CircleSlash`). An issue Trama cannot find on GitHub any more shows `Not found on GitHub` (muted, lucide `CircleHelp`) and only `Open on GitHub`.
- Signals, under the title of the row, as small outlined badges with an icon followed by a short reason in secondary text, in this order: `Stale` (amber, lucide `Clock`), `The Model changed after publishing`; `Carry-over` (amber, lucide `ArrowRight`), `From <sprint>`; `Overload` (destructive, lucide `Gauge`), `<first name> <load> / <capacity> d`; `Dependency` (muted, lucide `Link`), `Blocked by #<n>` (or `Blocked by <title>` when the dependency has no number). A closed or not planned issue shows no signal.
- Actions, aligned right, besides `Open on GitHub` (which becomes icon-only, lucide `ExternalLink`, tooltip `Open on GitHub`, when another action is there): `Comment update` (`outline`, lucide `MessageSquare`) on a stale open issue; `Scan` (`outline`, lucide `ScanSearch`) on a closed issue. Both are disabled, with the reason as tooltip, while their job runs (`Updating…`, `Scanning…`), while a Run launched from this window is in progress (`A run is in progress`) or with Team not available.
- The group row of an epic adds, after `<n> issues · <days> d`, ` · <closed> closed` when some are closed.
- Issue preview of a handoff (`ui#proposal/Open`): the `Sprint` field shows under its value, for the assignee, `<first name> has <load> / <capacity> d in this sprint` in secondary text, in the destructive colour when it goes over capacity. The load counts the open items of the linked Project assigned to that person in that sprint (`rules#issue/Published`), this issue, and the other issues of every open handoff (this handoff and stale ones included, never a Draft) assigned to that person in that sprint, each with the Manager's values applied.

## github-project/Linked
- Timeline tab, header row: a segmented toggle `By person` / `By epic` (default `By person`, kept while the panel is open) and, at the right in secondary text, `Block width = estimate (full width = 5 days)`.
- When the Project lacks sprints (`rules#github-project/Linked`), a warning box: lucide `TriangleAlert`, `The Project has no iteration after <sprint>.`, and `Add iterations on GitHub` (`outline`, lucide `ExternalLink`), which opens the Project's settings page in the browser.
- The grid sits in a bordered card: a first column, about 220 px, with the lanes, then one column per sprint shown (`rules#github-project/Linked`), each with the sprint title in medium weight and its dates in secondary text (`26–30 Oct`); the current sprint has the `Current` primary `Badge` next to its title and its cells on a light primary background. A last column `No iteration` with `After <date>` holds the open issues without sprint, its cells with a faint diagonal hatch.
- By person: one lane per member of the team, ordered by name, with the initials avatar, the name and `<capacity> d / sprint` in secondary text; then, when there are any, a lane `Unassigned` and a lane `Not in this team` for issues assigned to others. By epic: one lane per epic with its chip and `<n> issues`, in the order of the Issues tab.
- In a cell, one block per issue of that lane in that sprint, stacked in the order of dependencies: a bordered `rounded-md` block on the card background with the epic's coloured dot, `#<n>` in monospace, the title cut with an ellipsis and the estimate `<d> d`; its width is the estimate over 5 days of the cell's width, at least the width of `#<n>`. Its tooltip and accessible name are `#<n> <title> · <d> d · <status>`; clicking it opens the issue on GitHub. A closed issue's block is muted with a line through the number; an issue with a signal has a left border in the signal's colour.
- By person, absences are blocks with a dashed border and a faint hatch, lucide `CalendarOff` and `Off <dates>`, as wide as the working days they take in the sprint.
- By person, at the bottom right of each cell, in monospace and secondary text, `<load> / <capacity> d`, in the destructive colour and followed by ` · over` when the load is over capacity. By epic, the cells show `<days> d` of the epic in that sprint.
- Narrower than 720 px the grid scrolls sideways inside its card, with the lane column fixed; the page never scrolls sideways.
- Without a linked Project the tab shows the `Link a GitHub Project` page (`ui#github-project`); with no published issue, `No published issues yet: the timeline fills as handoffs are approved.` in secondary text above an empty grid of the sprints.
- Accessibility: the grid is a `table` with header cells for sprints and lanes; each load value is also read as `<load> of <capacity> days, over capacity` when over. Colours are never the only sign: signals have text, the current sprint has its badge.
- These texts are in English and do not follow `trama.language`.
