# Give the panel each Drift's two sides and the reason its Proposal cannot be approved

Every open Drift the panel receives carries its What happens text, its What should happen text, whether it points only to Requirements, and, when it has an attached Proposal, the state of that Proposal and the reason it cannot be approved, including that it is stale, invalid or waiting for other Proposals, which today go unnoticed.
